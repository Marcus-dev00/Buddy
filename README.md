<h1 align="center">Buddy</h1>

**Buddy** is a minimalist desktop AI companion for Windows. It lives quietly as a floating black pill on your screen—listening to your music, napping when you step away, and expanding into an AI chat & desktop assistant whenever you click it or drop a file.

## Download

Get the latest Windows installer from the **[Releases](https://github.com/Marcus-dev00/Buddy/releases/latest)** page. Built-in auto-updates keep Buddy up to date automatically.

## What Buddy Can Do

- **Lives Anywhere on Your Screen** — Drag Buddy freely across monitors or snap it magnetically to the top, bottom, left, or right edge. Its face and expanded panel automatically orient toward your screen.
- **Lively Expressions & Music Rhythm** — Responds smoothly to hover gaze, naps after idle time, and dances gently with system audio playback without shaking your chat window.
- **Multi-Model AI Chat & Thinking Depth** — Connects to OpenAI-compatible, Anthropic, and Gemini models with an adjustable 5-level thinking slider (`Off` to `Max`) and collapsible reasoning steps.
- **Desktop Agent & File Drag-and-Drop** — Drop files, images, or folders directly onto Buddy to analyze, edit, organize, or run tasks with your explicit approval before any changes.
- **7-Day Memory & Quick Snip** — Quietly indexes clipboard snippets and screenshots for 7 days (with full-text search and permanent starring), plus instant screen capture via `Alt + Shift + S`.
- **Private & Local** — API keys are encrypted locally using Windows DPAPI, and all chat history and memories stay on your device.
